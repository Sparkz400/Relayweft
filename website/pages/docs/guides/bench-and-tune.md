`rw bench` measures routed `rw` against single agents on your own tasks, and `rw tune` turns your logs into routing changes.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> It is not shown yet that routing beats a single agent. On the small starter tasks a single agent was faster and cheaper. The open question is real multi-file work: run `rw bench` on your own tasks to find out for your repo. The [benchmark results](../project/benchmarks/_index.md) so far are public.
