`rw` works with **GitHub**, **GitLab**, **Gitea or Forgejo**, **Bitbucket Cloud** and **Azure DevOps**: it turns issues or work items into tasks, finished tasks into pull requests, and follows up on review comments and failed checks.

See [Bitbucket Cloud](bitbucket.md) and [Azure DevOps](azure-devops.md) for their token setup, differences and validation status.
