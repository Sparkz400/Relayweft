These are the measurements so far, as they were written down after each run. They are small and use Claude only.

What they show: every run passed, and on the starter set's small, one-file tasks a single agent is faster and cheaper than the routed pipeline. Cost-aware model tiers changed almost nothing on tasks that small. What they do not show: whether routing pays off on bigger, multi-file tasks. That comparison is still open (see the [roadmap](../roadmap.md)). To measure it on your own work, use [`rw bench`](../../guides/bench-and-tune.md).
